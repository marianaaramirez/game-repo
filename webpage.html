Gameplay

Think fast. Choose wisely. Smash with math.

Math Smash is a strategic roguelike deck-building game made for kids. Before battles, players prepare a deck with attack, defense and skill cards. During combat, they solve math problems to activate each card.

The game rewards accuracy, strategy and quick thinking. Every run changes the map, enemies, rewards and possible paths, making each playthrough feel different.

Dynamic Runs Different maps, enemies, chests and rewards.
Math Combat Cards activate through timed math problems.
Deck Strategy Choose cards before battles and adapt your plan.
Progression Unlock skill cards and improve through each run.

Worlds

Three maps. Three levels of challenge.

Each world has a different mood, enemies and math difficulty. The player must progress from simple operations to mixed operations while building stronger strategies.

Mysterious • Adventurous

Ancient Temple

The Ancient Temple is the first world of the game. It introduces the player to the main combat system, card activation and timer mechanics. The environment uses ruins, plants and stone platforms to create an adventurous mood.

  • Math level: addition and subtraction
  • Enemies: slime, golem and predator plant
  • Goal: learn how cards, answers and timer power work

Tense • Dangerous

Castle

The Castle is a more dangerous world where the player faces stronger enemies and needs better strategy. The darker environment makes battles feel more intense and pushes the player to use defense and skill cards more carefully.

  • Math level: multiplication and division
  • Enemies: skeleton, spider and evil bat
  • Goal: survive stronger enemies with better deck choices

Hostile • Chaotic

Wasteland

The Wasteland is the hardest world. It combines different types of operations, stronger enemies and a more chaotic environment. This zone tests everything the player has learned during the run.

  • Math level: mixed operations
  • Enemies: predator plant and surprise enemies
  • Goal: defeat the final boss using the strongest deck strategy

Cards

Your deck is your strategy.

Before each combat, players prepare their deck. Attack cards deal damage, defense cards reduce incoming damage, and skill cards give special advantages such as freezing time or doubling card power.

Attack Cards

Quick Jab, Temple Strike, Vampiric Bite, Reckless Swing, Lucky Strike, Poison Dart, Knight Slash, Blood Saber, Venom Blade. (18 cards)

Defense Cards

Iron Wall, Phantom Cloak, Mending Barrier, Mirror Plate, Shadow Step, Spiked Rampart, Mirror Plate. (24 cards)

Skill Cards

Second Chance, Freeze Time, Clear Mind, Double Power and Vitality Boost.

Characters

Choose your hero before the run begins.

The player can choose between three different character skins. This gives the game a more personal feeling before entering the map and starting the first battle.

  • Three playable character skins.
  • Pixel-art style with bright colors and readable shapes.
  • Designed to be friendly and clear for younger players.

Level Select

Progress through harder math challenges.

Difficulty increases depending on the selected map. Easy maps focus on addition and subtraction, medium maps use multiplication and division, and hard maps combine different operations.

Easy: + and − Medium: × and ÷ Hard: mixed
